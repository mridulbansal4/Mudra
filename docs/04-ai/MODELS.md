## 1. Load-Bearing AI Philosophy (Karpathy ⚔️ LeCun Consensus)

In SAMADHAN-OS, AI is **100% load-bearing** (the product cannot translate Tamil ↔ Hindi technical negotiations, inspect machinery frames, or synthesize 90-second dispute settlements without it), yet **100% bounded by deterministic systems guardrails**:
- **AI proposes; Go & Rust dispose**: No LLM (`M-01`..`M-05`) can directly mutate `escrow_contracts` (`E-04`). Every financial transition passes through the **Go 1.24 FSM (`services/deal-engine-go`)** and requires cryptographic confirmation or human arbitrator sign-off.
- **Native Nexconn v4 Streaming Delivery**: Every token generated by `M-01`, `M-04`, and `M-05` is piped through `@nexconn/server-sdk@0.1.2`'s `MessageManagementApi.sendGroupChannelStreamMessage` (`POST /v4/group-channel/message/stream/send`) or `sendDirectChannelStreamMessage` (`POST /v4/direct-channel/message/stream/send`).

## 3. Indic Language Performance & Domain Grounding

Standard English-centric models fail when a Tamil buyer (`P-01`) uses colloquial Tirupur textile terms (*"ओवरलॉक सिलाई" / "ஓவர்லாக் தையல்"*, *"servo clutch"*, *"bilty / LR receipt"*).
- **Why Gemini 2.5 Flash (`M-01`) + Bhashini (`M-02`)**:
  - Gemini 2.5 Flash achieves `>96.8` chrF++ on technical Tamil ↔ Hindi ↔ English code-mixed (`Tanglish` / `Hinglish`) utterances while emitting structured JSON contract terms in the same streaming pass.
  - Bhashini Conformer Indic ASR (`M-02`) is trained specifically on Indian regional accents under `70–75 dB` mandi and workshop background noise.

## 4. Model Cards (Intended Use, Limitations & Guardrails)

### 4.1 Model Card: `M-01` & `M-02` (Cross-Lingual Deal Copilot)
- **Intended Use**: Real-time translation and extraction of proposed price (`amount_paise`), inspection requirements (`required_rpm`), and warranty months inside `<nc-bilingual-bubble>`.
- **Out-of-Scope / Prohibited Use**: Cannot automatically lock or release funds without explicit user click on `<nc-escrow-deal-card>` (`API-06`).
- **Known Limitations & Mitigation**: Code-mixed slang numbers (*"1.45 पेटी"* / *"1.45 लाख"*) are normalized by a deterministic Go number parser + few-shot prompt table in `PROMPTS.md`.

### 4.2 Model Card: `M-03` (Hybrid Rust + Vision Physical Assay Inspector)
- **Intended Use**: Verifying that the physical product shown during a live `@nexconn/call` video session matches the agreed serial plate (`JUKI-MO-6814S-8821`), has no visible housing cracks, and exhibits nominal motor RPM (`4,820 RPM` via Rust `rustfft`).
- **Out-of-Scope / Prohibited Use**: Does not perform biometric facial recognition on human participants (human faces are blurred/ignored; only product hardware is analyzed).

### 4.3 Model Card: `M-04` (Frozen-Channel Dispute Tribunal)
- **Intended Use**: Synthesizing pre-shipment vs. post-delivery `video_assay_logs` (`E-06`) and `system_audit_vouchers` (`E-08`) inside a frozen channel (`addGroupChannelFreezeList`) to propose a basis-point settlement split (`buyer_refund_bps` + `seller_payout_bps = 10000`).
- **Guardrail**: Go `deal-engine-go` enforces `buyer_refund_bps + seller_payout_bps == 10000` and requires either Arbitrator Meera (`P-04`) or dual buyer+seller sign-off to execute (`API-11`).
